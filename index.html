📖 Official Taboo Rules

🎯 Objective

Your team must guess as many target words as possible before the timer runs out!

🚫 What You CANNOT Do

  • You cannot say the Target Word or any form/derivative of it (e.g., if the word is "BICYCLE", you cannot say "BIKE" or "CYCLING").
  • You cannot say any of the 5 Taboo Words listed below it.
  • No gestures, hand motions, sound effects, or mouth pantomimes.
  • No "sounds like", "rhymes with", or "starts with the letter X" clues.
  • No abbreviations or foreign translations of the forbidden words.

👀 The Opponents' Role (Watchers)

One player from the opposing team sits beside/behind the Clue Giver and watches the card. If the Clue Giver accidentally speaks any Taboo word, the watcher hits the TABOO BUZZER!

⌨️ Desktop Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Spacebar / Right Arrow: Correct (+1 pt)
  • B / Down Arrow: Taboo Foul (-1 pt)
  • S / Left Arrow: Skip (0 pt)
  • P / Escape: Pause / Resume Round
